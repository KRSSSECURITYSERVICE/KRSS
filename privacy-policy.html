KRSS Privacy Policy

KRISHNA RAM SECURITY SERVICES (KRSS) collects information submitted through job applications, service enquiries and account features, such as contact details, preferred location, qualifications and documents voluntarily uploaded.

How information is used

Information is used to review applications, respond to enquiries, coordinate recruitment and deliver requested services. Access should be limited to authorised KRSS personnel.

Documents and retention

Uploaded documents may contain personal information. KRSS should retain them only as long as required for the stated purpose and applicable law, and securely delete them when no longer needed.

Security and contact

KRSS uses access controls, but no internet system can guarantee absolute security. Do not upload unnecessary sensitive documents. For privacy requests, contact KRSS through its verified official contact channel.

Important: This starter policy must be reviewed and completed with KRSS's verified legal entity details, contact email, retention periods and applicable local legal requirements before public launch.
